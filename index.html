You're in Scan: start with a drawing

  1. Photograph a drawing on paper: flat, in daylight, the whole sheet in view, on a darker surface. Drop the photo anywhere on this page, paste it, or open it.
  2. Scan it: check the sheet's corners, the ink and the colour. Several drawings on one page are found and numbered.
  3. Make something from it: a logo, an icon set or a texture, each in its own tab. Fonts start from their own letter sheets, in the Font tab.
