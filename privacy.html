Hand Drawn

privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

In short

  • Hand Drawn sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Your photos and drawings are processed in your browser. They are never uploaded, sent or kept anywhere else.
  • Nothing is stored in your browser either, unless you allow it. The only thing Hand Drawn can keep is the conversion settings you save.
  • It loads nothing from other companies: the app, its fonts and its icons all come from the same place as this page.

Who is responsible

Kevin Patton, Studio Orpiment. Contact: support@orpiment.studio

Your photos and drawings

When you open, drop or paste a photo, it is read and processed by code running in your browser. It stays in the page's memory while the page is open and is gone when you close or reload it. Files you download (SVGs, PNGs, icon packs, fonts) are saved on your device by your browser; Hand Drawn keeps no copy.

Cookies

None. Hand Drawn sets no cookies, and nothing on this site does.

What can be stored in your browser

Hosting

Hand Drawn is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Like any web host, Vercel's servers handle each request for these pages and may record standard technical details of it (such as the IP address, time, requested page and browser) in their logs, to deliver the site and keep it secure; this may happen outside the EU. Vercel keeps these records under its own policy: see vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy. Hand Drawn itself sends nothing to Vercel or anyone else once the page has loaded, and we do not look at or use these logs.

Your rights

Hand Drawn holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for it to give you, correct or delete; what your browser keeps you control yourself, as above. You can still contact the person responsible with any question, and you have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If what Hand Drawn stores ever changes, this page changes with it, and the app asks for your permission again.
