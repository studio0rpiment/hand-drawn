# The explanation flags

Every flag that opens when the pointer rests on a tab, or a tab has keyboard focus. Edit the words here; the page reads this file when it is built.

Each flag is:

- a `###` line with its **title**, the small capitals at the top of the flag;
- on the next line, its **name** in backticks, which ties it to the element it explains. Don't change the name;
- then its **text**, one paragraph (line breaks inside it become spaces).

The flags describe the thing, not the person using it: no "you".

## The tabs

### Scan
`tab.scan`

Where every drawing starts. A photo of a drawing on paper is straightened, its light evened out and its ink picked out from the paper, in the ink's own colour. A page of several drawings is split into a numbered tray; the one picked there is what Logo, Icon and Texture work on.

### Logo
`tab.logo`

The scanned drawing traced into a vector: an SVG of clean curves that holds at any size. Smoothness and simplify set how closely the curves follow the hand; fine tuning straightens the drawing or thickens its line by a fraction of a pixel. The colours are the scan's, or its own.

### Icon
`tab.icon`

The drawing as a family of icons under one style: line, fill and tint versions, turns and mirrors, an optional container, light and dark colours, favicons and a sprite. A page of drawings becomes a whole set in the same style.

### Texture
`tab.texture`

The drawing printed with its hand-made quality kept: rubber stamp, risograph, halftone or the ink as drawn. Once on a canvas, or as a seamless repeating tile, at any size up to print at 300 dpi.

### Font
`tab.font`

A typeface from drawn letters. Two printed sheets of boxes, and an optional third for signs, are filled in, photographed and read. Spacing, kerning and accented letters are worked out after Hochuli and Cheng, and the font downloads as an OpenType file.
