# Scan

The words on the Scan tab: taking in the photo, the paper, the ink, the colour and the sheet of drawings.

How to edit: change the words after a key; don't change the key in backticks, which ties the words to their place in the app. A long text may run on over several lines; a blank line ends it. {name} is filled in by the app (a number, a name). **bold**, *emphasis* and [a link](https://…) work. The screens show most words in lower case; write them in sentence case here.

## Before a photo

`scan.title` Scan
`scan.open` Open a photo
`scan.sample` Try a sample

## Settings

`scan.settings` Scan settings

## Sheet

`scan.sheet` Sheet
`scan.sheet-on` Several drawings on this sheet
`scan.sheet.hint` For a page of icons, or of logo iterations: each drawing is found and numbered, row by row. Pick one in the tray; every output works on it. Small drawings found as one: lower the group marks limit.
`scan.gap` Group marks limit
`scan.found` {n} drawings found · working on {pick}
`scan.found-one` 1 drawing found · working on 1
`scan.found-none` 0 drawings found
`scan.drawing` Drawing {n}

## 1 · Paper

`scan.paper` 1 · Paper
`scan.paper.hint` Drag the corners onto the sheet's corners. A photo taken sideways or upside down: turn it a quarter at a time with ↻; the Rotation slider straightens a slight tilt.
`scan.detect` Find the sheet
`scan.whole` Use whole photo
`scan.turn` Rotation
`scan.turn-90` Turn a quarter clockwise

## 2 · Ink

`scan.ink` 2 · Ink
`scan.flatten` Even out light
`scan.level` Ink cut-off
`scan.weight` Line weight
`scan.speck` Remove specks under
`scan.trim` Trim edges
`scan.level-auto` Pick the cut-off for me

## 3 · Colour

`scan.colour` 3 · Colour
`scan.colour.hint` Read from the photo; every output uses these unless it is set to its own. Logo downloads stay transparent unless their background is filled on the Logo tab.
`scan.ink-h` Ink hue
`scan.ink-s` Ink saturation
`scan.ink-l` Ink lightness
`scan.paper-h` Paper hue
`scan.paper-s` Paper saturation
`scan.paper-l` Paper lightness
`scan.paper-on` Keep the paper (off: transparent)
`scan.measure` Read colours from the photo
