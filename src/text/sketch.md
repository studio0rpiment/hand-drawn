# Sketches

The words on the Sketches tab, top to bottom: the tab and its flag, the upload and the collection, adjusting the image, adjusting the drawing, the style settings (output kinds, colours, size), the downloads, and the previews (image, sketch, collection, compare). The scan step shares its words with the other tabs (scan.md).

How to edit: change the words after a key; don't change the key in backticks, which ties the words to their place in the app. A long text may run on over several lines; a blank line ends it. {name} is filled in by the app (a number, a name). **bold**, *emphasis* and [a link](https://…) work. The screens show most words in lower case; write them in sentence case here. The words never speak to the reader as "you".
