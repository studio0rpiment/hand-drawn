# Logo

The words on the Logo tab: the vector, fine tuning, colour and export.

How to edit: change the words after a key; don't change the key in backticks, which ties the words to their place in the app. A long text may run on over several lines; a blank line ends it. {name} is filled in by the app (a number, a name). **bold**, *emphasis* and [a link](https://…) work. The screens show most words in lower case; write them in sentence case here.

## Settings

`logo.settings` Logo settings

## Vector

`logo.vector` Vector
`logo.smooth` Smoothness
`logo.simplify` Simplify curves
`logo.pad` Space around

## Fine tuning

`logo.rotate` Rotate
`logo.spread` Line spread
`logo.ignore` Ignore spots under
`logo.tuning` Fine tuning
`logo.tuning.hint` On the vector only; the scan stays as it is.
`logo.square` Square artboard

## Colour

`logo.scan-colours` Colours from the scan
`logo.colour` Colour
`logo.ink` Ink
`logo.bg` Background
`logo.bg-on` Fill the background (off: transparent)

## Export

`logo.export` Export
`logo.dl-svg` Download SVG
`logo.copy-svg` Copy SVG
`logo.dl-png` Download PNG
`logo.dl-all` Download all SVGs
`logo.tracing` Tracing {n} of {total}…
`logo.packed` {n} SVGs packed
`logo.no-ink` No ink found. Try a higher ink cut-off.
`logo.stats` {width} × {height} px · {outlines} outlines · {segments} segments · {kb} KB

## The logo

`logo.heading` Logo
`logo.globes` Settings in pairs
